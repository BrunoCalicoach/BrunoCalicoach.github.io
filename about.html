My name is Bruno Žgela and I have dedicated more than 15 years to mastering bodyweight training, movement, strength and athletic development. My Journey

From a 13-year-old kid on the street workout bars to international podiums. Here is how it happened.

2013 · Age 13

Where it all started

I started my calisthenics journey in 2013, as a 13-year-old kid inspired by the freedom of street workout and the idea that you can build incredible strength using nothing more than your own body.

Coming from a background in sports, including football and wrestling, I developed the discipline, determination and competitive mindset that continue to guide me today.

The early days on the street workout bars.

The years of practice

No shortcuts

Along the way I learned that real progress doesn't come from shortcuts. It comes from consistency, intelligent programming and patience.

Throughout my journey I have always been driven by one goal: to keep pushing the limits of what the human body can achieve through bodyweight training. These are the principles I now teach every client.

Thousands of hours on the bar leave their marks.
