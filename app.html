How we work together

From your first login to your next personal best, the app is where it happens.

  1. 1

    Tell me about you

    When you first log in, a short starting form asks about your training, goals, injuries, schedule and eating habits, so I know exactly where you're starting from.

  2. 2

    Get your plan

    I write your training plan week by week. The moment I publish it, it's on your phone, with my notes on every exercise.

  3. 3

    Train and log

    Follow the plan, enter your reps, weight and effort, and leave a note. It works without wifi and syncs when you're back online.

  4. 4

    Check in weekly

    Rate your week, add your measurements and ask your questions. Your weigh-ins and steps are already there.

  5. 5

    Get feedback, level up

    I read everything, reply on your workouts and adjust next week's plan, so every week builds on the last.

Everything inside

Weekly plan and full history

This week at a glance with a ring for workouts done, and every past week a tap away.

Set-by-set logging

Reps, weight and RPE for every set, with your previous results shown beside each exercise.

Rest timer

Starts when you log a set, turns green at your minimum rest, and vibrates and beeps when it's time.

Feedback on your workouts

My notes on each training day and on your whole week, right where you'll see them.

Weight that makes sense

Check-ins, measurements, photos

Calorie, macro and water targets

Calculated from your details and goal, then fine-tuned by me. A water tracker and daily steps included.
