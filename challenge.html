How it works

From your first test to your retest, the app guides every step.

  1. 1

    Test yourself

    Three guided testing days with 11 short tests: max reps of pull-ups, dips, push-ups and rows, plus beep tests for endurance. The app times everything.

  2. 2

    Get your plan

    Your 9-week plan is built from your own results, exercise by exercise, starting at a level you can handle and growing from there.

  3. 3

    Train, guided

    One clear screen per set: the exercise, your target, a demo video and a rest timer. EMOM rounds run themselves with a beep.

  4. 4

    Log in seconds

    Enter the reps you did, then rate your energy, sleep and effort. Your next workouts adjust immediately.

  5. 5

    Retest and level up

    After 9 weeks you take 2 to 3 days off, retest, see how far you've come, and start the next cycle from your new numbers.

A normal plan is written once and stays the same, whether it suits you or not. This one reads every workout you log, your reps plus your energy and sleep, and adjusts your next targets set by set. It steps up when you're ready, holds when you had a bad day, and resets to your real level when you miss on a good day.

Just enter what you did. If you slept badly or had low energy, the target stays the same. If you felt good and still missed, the plan takes that as your real level, sets the next target to match, and climbs back up from there. Two misses in a row bring a lighter week.

Tick "This was too easy" and the plan skips ahead a step. You may also go past your first-set target when you feel strong, and that higher number becomes your new starting point.
