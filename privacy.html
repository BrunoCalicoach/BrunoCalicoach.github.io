Privacy Policy

In short: I collect only what I need to coach you and to answer you. Your training, body and nutrition data are visible only to you and me. I don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics cookies. You can see, download or delete your data at any time by emailing coaching@brunozgela.com.

For anything about your data, email coaching@brunozgela.com. I reply within one month at the latest, usually much sooner.

2. What I collect, why, and on what legal basis

Visiting this website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub briefly records technical data such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Fonts (Google Fonts) and some code libraries (Tailwind CSS, Feather icons, Supabase's code library) are loaded from their providers' servers, which also receive your IP address so they can send the files.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running a working and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Applying for coaching or contacting me

When you send the application form, I receive your name, email and your answers (for example your training experience, goals, schedule and anything you choose to tell me about injuries or health). The form is delivered to my email by the service Web3Forms.

Legal basis: steps you ask for before we agree on coaching (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Health information you choose to include is processed with your explicit consent, given by sending it (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

Newsletter and waitlist

If you sign up for updates, I store your email, first name and the topics you picked with Brevo, my email service. Sign-up uses double opt-in: nothing is sent until you confirm by email. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

The coaching app (clients only)

If you are my coaching client, the app stores:

  • Account: your email and password. Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; I can never see them.
  • Initial form (filled in once in the app before coaching starts, and updated when you choose): your name, phone number (for WhatsApp) and the email you want to be contacted on; sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity level and goal, used to estimate your energy needs; your training history, schedule, place of training and equipment; your goals; current and past injuries and any medical conditions or medication you tell me about; your eating habits, and for Coaching PRO also your diet type, food allergies and intolerances, foods you don't eat, cooking habits and supplements; and your usual daily routine (work type, wake-up and bedtime).
  • Training: your weekly plans, the results you log (reps, weights, effort), your workout notes and my feedback.
  • Progress: daily body weight, body measurements, daily steps, weekly check-ins (ratings of training, nutrition, stress, sleep, satisfaction and motivation, and your comments) and progress photos.
  • Nutrition: your calorie and macro targets, your meal plan and the swaps you make, and any allergies or foods to avoid, your daily water intake, and foods you add yourself (for example local products you scan).

I use this only to coach you: to plan your training and nutrition, follow your progress and give you feedback.

Legal basis: our coaching agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Body weight, measurements, photos, injuries, medical conditions, allergies and anything else about your health are special categories of data, which I process only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You give it in the app before anything is stored, and you can withdraw it at any time by emailing me. After that I delete that data, and coaching continues without the features that need it.

Who can see it: only you and me. The database enforces this: every client can read and change only their own data, and progress photos are kept in private storage that only you and I can open.

Calculations: the app estimates your energy needs and suggests meal swaps with standard formulas and nutrition data. These are aids for us, not automated decisions about you: I make the coaching decisions, together with you.

Data kept on your device

The app keeps your login and a copy of your plan and recent entries on your phone or computer, so it also works without internet in the gym. This is strictly necessary for the app to work, so it doesn't need a cookie banner. Logging out removes this copy from the device.

Some providers are based in the USA or India. Where personal data leaves the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long I keep your data

  • Coaching app data: while you are my client and for 12 months after our last coaching period, so you can come back and continue where you left off. Then it is deleted. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.
  • Progress photos: deleted when coaching ends, unless you ask me to keep them for a return.
  • Applications that don't lead to coaching: up to 12 months.
  • Newsletter and waitlist: until you unsubscribe.
  • Invoices and payment records (once payments start): as long as Croatian accounting and tax law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • see the data I hold about you and get a copy (access);
  • have it corrected (rectification);
  • have it deleted (erasure);
  • limit how I use it (restriction);
  • receive it in a common file format to take elsewhere (portability);
  • object to processing based on my legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Just email coaching@brunozgela.com. If you think I'm not handling your data correctly, you can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority: Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr.

7. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only in hashed form. In the database, access rules ensure every client can reach only their own data, photos are in private storage, and only my coach account can see clients' data.

8. Age

Athletes under 18 can be coached only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who agrees to the coaching and to how the data is processed. For athletes under 16, consent to processing personal data must be given by the parent or guardian (Croatian law sets 16 as the age of digital consent). Please don't send me a minor's data without a parent's or guardian's agreement.

9. Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle your data, I update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects coaching clients, I tell you before it applies.
